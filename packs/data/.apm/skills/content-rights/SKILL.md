# Content rights skill

Use this skill to assess whether a website may be used as a source for internal enterprise news monitoring and AI summarization.

## Standards

- Do not provide legal advice; collect and classify evidence only
- Never treat robots.txt as a license; Terms of Use override it for risk assessment
- Quote relevant text exactly and give the source URL for every finding
- Note conflicting evidence and be conservative when rights are unclear

## Common expectations

- review `/robots.txt`, `/ai.txt`, terms, copyright, licensing, AI/TDM, and API policies
- classify each category as ALLOWED, RESTRICTED, PROHIBITED, NOT_ADDRESSED, or UNCLEAR
- do not assume AI use is allowed when it is not mentioned
- score risk as LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH and list licensing or permission contacts as the preferred compliance path
- finish with a concise executive summary for legal, procurement, and engineering teams

# Performance skill

Use this skill for diagnosing and improving performance.

## Standards

- Measure before and after every change
- Target the proven bottleneck, not guesses
- Prefer simple fixes over complex optimizations
- Avoid premature caching without an invalidation strategy

## Common expectations

- benchmarks or load tests with stated conditions
- N+1 and async pitfalls checked
- impact and trade-offs documented
